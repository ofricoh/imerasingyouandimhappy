[12] INT. CLEMENTINE'S APARTMENT – NIGHT (Joel sits in the living room, somewhat nervously. He looks at the books on her shelves. Clementine is in the kitchen preparing drinks and chatting. Clementine is in the living room now with two gin and tonics) CLEMENTINE Two blue ruins... JOEL Thank you. CLEMENTINE Drink up, young man. It'll make the whole seduction part less repugnant. (Clementine starts laughing) CLEMENTINE I'm just kidding! Come on. (Joel sits in a chair across the room. Clementine finishes her drink) CLEMENTINE You're kinda closed-mouthed, aren't you? JOEL I'm sorry. It's just, you know, my life isn't that interesting. I go to work, I come home. Don't know what to say. You should read my journal. I mean, it's just... blank. CLEMENTINE Really? Does that make you sad or anxious? I mean, I'm always anxious, thinking I'm not living my life to the fullest, taking advantage of every possibility, making sure I'm not wasting one second of the little time I have. JOEL I think about that. CLEMENTINE Yeah? (They laugh awkwardly) CLEMENTINE You're really nice. JOEL Ohh! CLEMENTINE God, I have to stop saying that. I'm gonna marry you. I know it. JOEL Um... okay. CLEMENTINE Joel, you should come up to the Charles with me sometime. JOEL That sounds scary. CLEMENTINE Exactly. I'll pack a picnic. A night picnic. Night picnics are different. And, um, we could... JOEL Sounds good. But I... I should, uh, go. Now. CLEMENTINE You should stay. JOEL No, I really... I'm... I... I have to get up so early. (Joel puts on his overcoat. Clementine heads to the phone table, grabs a pen) CLEMENTINE I would like you to call me. Would you do that? I would like it. (She scribbles her phone number on Joel's right hand) JOEL Yes. (Joel exits. Clementine watches him through an open window as Joel gets in his car) CLEMENTINE Wish me a happy Valentine's Day when you call! That'd be nice.
